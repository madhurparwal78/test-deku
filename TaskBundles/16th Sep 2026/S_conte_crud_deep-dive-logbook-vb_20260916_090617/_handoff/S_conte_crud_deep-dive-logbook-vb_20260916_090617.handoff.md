## WHAT THE ORACLE MUST GET RIGHT FIRST

1. **The order write.** `PUT /api/logbook/order` takes the complete list and rewrites every
   position from its index. A missing, repeated or foreign identifier is refused and the stored
   order is left alone. Two racing writes must land as one whole list.
2. **The two identifiers.** `publicId` is minted once, is not the row identifier, survives an
   unpublish, and a draft is never reachable through it. The page route accepts both forms.
3. **The host boundary.** The host asking for a draft is answered not found, identically for a
   real draft and an invented identifier.
4. **Mail.** Four messages with pinned subjects, the live message carrying the public address,
   and nothing on unpublish, answer, reorder or sign-in. A reset for an unknown address sends
   nothing and answers exactly as a known one does.
5. **The gate.** Every browser workflow passes the entry gate first; `Enter without audio` has
   to be a real control that enters.
