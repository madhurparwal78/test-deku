## What the operator should read first

`_handoff/S_ecomm_comm_travel-luggage-storefront-vb_20260916_121653.report.md` carries the gate log, the slot obligations, the rubric budget and the
literals ledger. Three decisions in it are worth knowing before the app is built:

1. **Money and stock must agree under pressure.** The checkout re-reads every price and refuses
   with `price_changed` when the sale ended after the item was added; the stock check and the
   `sale` movements happen in the same transaction that marks the order paid; `checkout_key` is
   unique, so a repeat is the same order; and two checkouts for the last unit of `RDG-TRL-SFL`
   give one paid order and one `out_of_stock` refusal.
2. **Paid means killbill holds the account.** Every paid order is billed to `valisette-<number>`,
   opened with `POST /1.0/kb/accounts` and read back by `externalKey` before the order turns
   `paid`. Corporate checkouts read the seeded account instead and refuse unknown or EUR accounts.
   Invoices and amounts are not graded at the provider (grader 0.22.0 exposes accounts only).
3. **The front end is graded in depth.** 68 browser substeps walk the rail, panels, search overlay,
   collection filters, product page, cart, checkout, account and store directory, and 54 judged
   criteria cover the ruled grid, the black rail, the amber ticker, drawn pictures, motion, phone
   layout and WCAG 2.2 AA. Every picture and icon is drawn by the page; no image, font or video
   file may be fetched.
