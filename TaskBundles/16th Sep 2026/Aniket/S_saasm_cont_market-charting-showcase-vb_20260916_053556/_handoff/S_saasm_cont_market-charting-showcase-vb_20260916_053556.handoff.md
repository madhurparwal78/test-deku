## What the downstream builder must know

The bundle's critical focus is the draft boundary. The reference app has to put the
chart snapshot bytes in the MinIO bucket under `ideas/{idea_id}/{sha256_of_bytes}.{ext}`
and answer a draft idea, and its snapshot, to the owning author alone. An app that
keeps the image on its own disk, or that hides the draft in the interface while the
address still answers, passes a browser walk and fails the graders.

The second graded trap is the publish: a repeated publish must leave exactly one
published idea and one stored object, and two publishes arriving together must accept
exactly one. The brief states the observable rule and names no mechanism, which is
deliberate at variant `b`.

## Self-attestation, recorded rather than assumed

Every prompt receipt in `_handoff/<code>.receipts.json` records `verifier: self`. The
kit's rule is owner != verifier, so the QC verdicts in this run are recorded verdicts,
never independent ones. `prompt_receipt_lint.py` prints a SELF-ATTESTED note for each,
and G40 exits 0 with verdict WARN for that reason. An independent reviewer re-running
the seven certification prompts is the thing that turns those notes into findings or
into confirmations.
