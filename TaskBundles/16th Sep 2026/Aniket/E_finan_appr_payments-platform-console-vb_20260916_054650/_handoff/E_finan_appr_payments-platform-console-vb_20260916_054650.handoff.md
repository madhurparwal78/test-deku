## What the downstream builder must know before writing the app

**The browser pass runs before the pytest pass, and this bundle is authored around that.**
The strategy used is DISJOINT SEED ENTITIES (reference/J J.12, reference/E E.6). Browser
journeys raise and approve a refund request on `pay_kaufhaus_200000` and otherwise read.
The pytest layer never touches that payment: it refunds `pay_corvid_12345`,
`pay_streamly_4500` and `pay_beanbar_2280`, and raises every request against
`pay_northwind_75000`. Absolute-value assertions are confined to `northbeam`/`sandbox` and
`wellspring`/`live`, which no browser substep mutates; every assertion on
`northbeam`/`live` is relational (a delta, a sum, or a zero-sum invariant) so it holds
after the browser pass has moved money.

**The refund-request lifetime is env-injected.** `REFUND_REQUEST_TTL_SEC` is `86400` in
`[environment].env` and `8` in `[verifier].env`. The app must READ it; a duration compiled
into the app makes `test_expired_request_cannot_be_approved` unsatisfiable, and the whole
seam exists so the verifier can shrink a twenty-four-hour wait without the agent knowing.

**Expiry is lazy.** The kit's baseline forbids cron, workers and queues, so an expired
request is computed as expired the next time it is read. That is externally
indistinguishable from a sweeper and is what the graders observe.
