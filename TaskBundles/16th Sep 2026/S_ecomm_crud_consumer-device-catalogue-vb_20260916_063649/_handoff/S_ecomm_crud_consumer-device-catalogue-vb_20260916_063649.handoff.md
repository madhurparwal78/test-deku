## What the operator should read first

1. `instruction.md` is the only file the agent sees. Everything else here is held out.
2. `solution/checklist.md` is the coverage target the reference app is built from.
   234 items, every one cited by a grader, proven in both directions by
   `coverage_map.py` (G24).
3. `tests/traceability-matrix.md` maps each core ask to the checks that grade it. It
   is regenerated by every sweep, so it cannot disagree with the gate.
4. `_handoff/<code>.report.md` carries the full gate log, the literals ledger summary
   and the grading-window measurement.

## The two things most likely to go wrong downstream

**The buy route.** Every variant carries one of three fulfilment modes, resolved on
the server. The seeded catalogue carries all three, and seven of the fourteen variants
are handed off. A reference app that implements only the direct route will pass its own
reading of the brief and fail `test_handoff_variant_exposes_outbound_route_not_a_cart_add`
and `test_outbound_click_row_is_recorded_without_an_order`.

**The dot layer.** Every route carries one fixed field of dots painted in an inverting
blend, and two routes exist specifically to prove it: the operating system release route
is near black and the community grant route is saturated pink. A build that hard codes
the dot colour looks correct on the home route and disappears on both of those.
