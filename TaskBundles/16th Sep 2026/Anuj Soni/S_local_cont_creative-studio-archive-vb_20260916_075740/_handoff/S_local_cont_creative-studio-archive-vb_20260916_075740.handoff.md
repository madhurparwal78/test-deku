## What the operator should read first

`_handoff/S_local_cont_creative-studio-archive-vb_20260916_075740.report.md` carries the gate log,
the slot obligations, the rubric budget and the literals ledger. Two decisions in it are worth
knowing before the app is built:

1. **The publication gate is the task.** A case reaches `published` only when a client approval is
   recorded against the exact revision being published, a lead has approved that same revision,
   every required field is present and every referenced image is `ready`. The conditions are
   rechecked at the moment of the transition, so an edit between approval and publication must fail.
2. **The draft boundary holds in four places.** The case route, the list that feeds the stack, the
   cover object in MinIO, and the review token that is deliberately let through exactly one of them.
   An app that hides a draft in the interface alone fails three of the four.
